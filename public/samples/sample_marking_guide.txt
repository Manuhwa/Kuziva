MARKING GUIDE - Educational Psychology Mid-Term Examination

Total: 40 marks

Question 1: Zone of Proximal Development (10 marks)

- Clear definition of ZPD as the gap between what a learner can do independently and what they can do with guidance (3 marks)
- Explanation of how ZPD relates to teaching and learning (2 marks)
- Methods to identify ZPD: observation, assessment, diagnostic testing (2 marks)
- Practical classroom strategies to work within ZPD: peer tutoring, guided practice, differentiated instruction (3 marks)

Question 2: Piaget vs Bruner (15 marks)

- Piaget's stages of cognitive development explained: sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operational, formal operational (4 marks)
- Bruner's spiral curriculum and three modes of representation: enactive, iconic, symbolic (4 marks)
- Key differences: stage-based vs continuous learning, biological maturation vs cultural tools (3 marks)
- Educational implications: age-appropriate activities (Piaget), revisiting concepts at increasing complexity (Bruner) (4 marks)

Question 3: Scaffolding Examples (8 marks)

- Definition of scaffolding as temporary support that is gradually removed (2 marks)
- Example 1: Using manipulatives before abstract symbols (2 marks)
- Example 2: Think-aloud modeling of problem-solving steps (2 marks)
- Example 3: Providing worked examples, then partially completed problems, then independent practice (2 marks)

Question 4: Motivation in Learning (7 marks)

- Role of motivation: drives engagement, persistence, and learning outcomes (2 marks)
- Intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation clearly differentiated with examples (2 marks)
- Strategies to enhance intrinsic motivation: autonomy, mastery, purpose, choice, relevance, feedback (3 marks)
