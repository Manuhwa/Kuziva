Educational Psychology Mid-Term Examination

Subject: Educational Psychology
Date: November 2024
Time: 2 hours
Total Marks: 40

Instructions: Answer all questions. Write clearly and provide examples where appropriate.

Question 1: Explain Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) and discuss how a teacher can identify and utilize a learner's ZPD in the classroom. (10 marks)

Question 2: Compare and contrast Piaget's and Bruner's theories of cognitive development. Discuss the educational implications of each theory. (15 marks)

Question 3: What is scaffolding in the context of learning? Provide three practical examples of scaffolding techniques that a primary school teacher could use in a mathematics lesson. (8 marks)

Question 4: Discuss the role of motivation in learning. Explain the difference between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, and provide strategies a teacher can use to enhance intrinsic motivation in students. (7 marks)
